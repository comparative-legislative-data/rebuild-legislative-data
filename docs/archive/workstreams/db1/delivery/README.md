# DB1 delivery archive

This directory retains the detailed DB1 delivery packets produced during the
initial implementation journey. They are authoritative evidence of what was
proposed, approved, attempted, captured, tested or accepted at the time; they
are not the current reading path and do not themselves authorise new work.

Start with the live [DB1 workstream narrative](../../../../workstreams/db1/README.md)
and the [GB-SCT source-control guide](../../../../data/gb-sct/README.md).

## Contents

- **Foundation and proof (DEC-0075–DEC-0082):** synthetic raw/projection
  proof, first source batch, first explorer, reference/institutional cohorts,
  and the access-direction record.
- **Collection and annual-window delivery (DEC-0083–DEC-0097):** formal
  stages, Bills, roles, parties, member context, committees, MQA collections,
  and Questions/Votes annual windows.
- **Official Reports and interim access (DEC-0098–DEC-0100):** the 2025
  cohort, 1999–2024/2026 expansion, and the now-superseded interim
  researcher-access proposal/design.
- **Superseded usability delivery (DEC-0102–DEC-0105):** the first
  researcher-workspace direction and implementation, owner-testing
  accessibility corrections, retained-response assembly correction, and the
  external UX commission. Their lasting lessons are consolidated in the live
  [DB1 narrative](../../../../workstreams/db1/README.md) and the later
  [DEC-0106 endpoint-workspace proposal](DEDICATED_ENDPOINT_WORKSPACE_PROPOSAL_DEC0106.md).

The current DB1 narrative records the lessons from these packets, including
the distinction between raw retained responses and operational projections,
the 2006 Committee Official Reports availability response, and the agreed
research-product reset.
